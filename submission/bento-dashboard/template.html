Methods and limits

Transparent enough to rerun

The dataset, taxonomy, manual labels, validation results and scripts are included with the submission.

How the analysis was produced

I used Node.js and google-play-scraper to collect 600 newest and 400 helpful English-language India reviews, then deduplicated them to 910 unique records. I manually reviewed and classified 160 reviews using a documented primary-theme taxonomy. The recent complaint subset is directional rather than a population estimate. A keyword classifier achieved only 43.3% exact agreement on a frozen validation set, so its aggregate totals were rejected. Version comparisons are descriptive, not causal.
